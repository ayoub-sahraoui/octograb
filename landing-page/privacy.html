Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 13, 2026

1. Introduction

OctoGrab ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use the OctoGrab Chrome extension and our website.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Extension Data

OctoGrab operates entirely within your browser. We do not collect, transmit, or store any of the following data on our servers:

  • Web pages you visit or scrape
  • Data you extract using blueprints
  • Blueprint configurations you create
  • Any content from the websites you interact with

All extracted data, blueprints, and settings are stored locally in your browser's IndexedDB storage and never leave your device.

2.2 Purchase Information

When you purchase a license, we collect:

  • Email address — to deliver your license key and provide support
  • Payment information — processed securely by Stripe. We never see or store your full credit card number.
  • Name — as provided during checkout

2.3 Website Analytics

Our website may use privacy-respecting analytics (e.g., Plausible or similar) to understand visitor traffic. These analytics:

  • Do not use cookies
  • Do not track individual users
  • Do not collect personal information
  • Only record aggregate page views and referrer data

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for:

  • Delivering and activating your license
  • Providing customer support
  • Sending important product updates (you can opt out at any time)
  • Processing refund requests
  • Improving our website and product

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties, except:

  • Stripe — our payment processor, which handles payment transactions under their own privacy policy
  • Legal requirements — if required by law or to protect our rights

5. Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your information:

  • All website communications are encrypted via HTTPS/TLS
  • Payment processing is handled by PCI-compliant Stripe
  • We minimize data collection to only what's necessary
  • Extension data never leaves your browser

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data we hold
  • Correct inaccurate personal data
  • Delete your personal data (email us to request deletion)
  • Opt out of marketing emails at any time
  • Export your extension data locally (built into the extension)

7. Chrome Extension Permissions

OctoGrab requests the following Chrome permissions:

  • sidePanel — to display the OctoGrab sidebar
  • activeTab — to interact with the current tab when running blueprints
  • scripting — to execute extraction scripts on web pages

These permissions are used solely for the extension's core functionality. We do not use them to collect or transmit your browsing data.

8. Children's Privacy

OctoGrab is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of OctoGrab after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at:
