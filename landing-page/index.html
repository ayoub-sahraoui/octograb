Features

Everything you need to
extract the web.

OctoGrab packs enterprise-grade scraping power into a friendly visual interface that lives right in your browser sidebar.

Visual Blueprint Builder

Drag-and-drop blocks to build scraping workflows visually. Navigate, click, wait, loop, extract — all without writing a single line of code.

Multi-Format Export

Export your extracted data instantly as CSV, JSON, or Excel (.xlsx) files. Ready for spreadsheets, databases, or APIs.

Smart Selectors

Use CSS selectors, XPath, text matching, or ARIA roles. OctoGrab finds elements reliably even on complex, dynamic pages.

Data Transformers

Clean extracted values with built-in transformers: regex extract/replace, trim, uppercase, lowercase, number parsing, and more.

Pagination & Loops

Automatically paginate through lists, loop over elements, and navigate across multiple pages — all handled by the engine.

Pause, Resume & Checkpoint

Long-running scrapes? Pause anytime and resume from where you left off. Your data and progress are saved automatically.

Formula & Static Fields

Add computed fields with math formulas, auto-increment IDs, UUIDs, timestamps, and constants — all without extra code.

Import & Export Blueprints

Share your scraping blueprints as JSON files. Import community blueprints or reuse your own across projects.

FAQ

Common questions,
straight answers.

Is OctoGrab free?

Yes, OctoGrab is completely free and open source. There are no premium tiers, usage limits, or hidden fees. You can use it for personal and commercial projects.

Does it work on any website?

OctoGrab works on most public websites. It runs in your actual browser tab, so if you can see the data, OctoGrab can extract it. It handles dynamic JavaScript-rendered content, SPAs, and paginated lists.

Do I need to know how to code?

Not at all. OctoGrab is designed for non-technical users. You build scraping workflows visually by adding blocks — Navigate, Click, Wait, Extract, etc. No scripting, no terminal, no APIs.

How is this different from web scraping tools like Scrapy or Puppeteer?

Those are code-first developer tools. OctoGrab is a visual, no-code Chrome extension. You build blueprints in a sidebar, not in a code editor. It's designed for speed and simplicity, not for massive infrastructure-scale scraping.

Can I scrape data that requires login?

Yes. Since OctoGrab runs in your actual browser session, it uses your existing cookies and authentication state. Just log in normally, then run your blueprint.

What export formats are supported?

CSV, JSON, and Excel (.xlsx). You can download your data at any time — even while a scrape is still running.

Can I pause and resume a long scraping job?

Yes! OctoGrab supports pause, resume, and checkpointing. Your progress and extracted data are preserved even if you close the browser.

Is my data sent to any server?

No. OctoGrab runs entirely in your browser. Your data never leaves your machine. There are no analytics, no telemetry, and no cloud dependencies.
